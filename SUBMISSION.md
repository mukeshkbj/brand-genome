## Short description

One kiln, three brands, fired from one recipe. Sunday Kiln, a small pottery studio in Accra, runs three storefront brands (Sunday Kiln, Night Market, Saturday Market) from one catalogue, one MoMo payment method and two AI agents. Each brand is a genome of design tokens. Potters record glazes by firing test tiles, so each home page shows its genome as glaze test tiles, and every product is drawn as a pot dipped in one of those glazes. The live store also reads today's Accra weather and moves rain-day pieces to the front.

## Built on Krifth

- 4 tools (exchange rates, Accra weather, phone alerts, Ghana holidays), 2 agents, 7 tasks
